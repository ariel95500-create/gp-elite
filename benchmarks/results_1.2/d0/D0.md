# D0, the typed start under units

Diagnostic of plan 1.2 (`../PLAN.md`, *Found during the prototypes*), run on
4 November 2026 before any change to the engine. It decides nothing. It ran
gp-elite 1.1.0 from PyPI, wrapped from outside without changing what it
draws (`d0_start.py`), one process per fit, `PYTHONHASHSEED=0`, on the
machine of `machine.txt`. That is the second machine of prototype 4, which
runs these fits faster than the first.

Records:
- `d0_start.jsonl`: 81 fits, no crash. They are U41 at seed 0 as
  `phase1_bench.py` runs it (30 s), and the units arm of the TP diagnostic at
  draw 0 under the API defaults.
- `d0_dims.json`: the exponents the units ask for, computed without any fit.
- `d0_fastpath.jsonl`: a replay, described below.
- `d0_cure.jsonl`: a prototype of the cure, described below.

Every figure below is in `d0_summary.txt`, the output of
`python d0_start.py summary`.

## How the typed start works in 1.1.0

Under `units=`, each of the three islands (133 places) is filled by
`dim_search.typed_random_tree`.

1. It first tries a fast path, `_monomial_tree`. This step solves by least
   squares the integer exponents e_i that make the product of the input
   units equal to the target unit. It then builds that product with a free
   coefficient. It accepts the product only when the sum of the |e_i| is at
   most 8 and each |e_i| at most 4.
2. Otherwise it runs a blind search, `_build`. This search builds random
   trees of the target unit, at depth 2 to 7. It makes up to 40 attempts,
   each of at most 4,000 steps.

A draw that fails returns None. `Island.initialize` then draws again, until
the island is full or 20 draws per place have failed, and completes the
island with copies of what it has. Nothing in this looks at the deadline.

## 1. Where the time of III.19.51 goes

The target is an energy, m·q⁴/(ε²·h²) with n dimensionless. Its exponents
are (1, 4, −2, −2), and their sum is 9. The fast path refuses this product
and never gives a tree.

- **Duration.** The start takes 443.5 s of a fit of 444.6 s, and the fit then
  runs one generation. On the first machine of the prototypes this was 600 to
  735 s.
- **Draws.** It takes 3,549 typed draws to fill 399 places. 3,150 of them
  return None (89 %), and they take 417.6 s of the 443.5 s (94 %). The
  islands end full of real trees: no copy is needed, at about 1.1 s per tree.
- **The blind search.** It is called 134,022 times. 111,203 of those calls
  run out of their 4,000 steps (83 %), and 399 build a tree (0.3 %). In all,
  that is 459 million steps of `_build_ek`.

The start has no other fault: the time is not lost in a loop, a repeated
computation or a numerical failure. It is the blind search, used where it
almost never succeeds, with a cost that has no bound in time: up to 20 × 133
failed draws per island, each of up to 160,000 steps.

## 2. III.19.51 is alone

In all the other 80 fits the start takes at most 0.56 s.

- **The spring.** Under units it is the slowest of these. Its √(m/k) has
  half exponents, so it goes through the blind search, which succeeds there.
- **The products in U41.** Of the 41 equations of U41, 30 ask for an exact
  product of integer powers (`d0_dims.json`). Their sums of exponents go from
  1 to 9. I.32.5 sits at the limit (8). Only III.19.51 is above it.

## 3. Why the draws are hard, and what the slow start brings

Nine factors, four of them the same charge, are rarely reached by random
trees of depth at most 7 in 4,000 steps. Those trees reach it through
squares (`(v1²)²`).

The slow start is also what returns the law. In the campaigns of 1.1
(`../../results_1.1/campaign/U41_*.jsonl`), III.19.51 under units is exact at
all ten seeds and near under noise, after a single generation: trees of the
start already have the structure of the law, n² included
(`-0.00316629 * v0 * (v1²)² / (v2² * v3² * v4²)`).

## What this means for the correction (b) as written

Correction (b) as written stops the start at the deadline and completes the
population with copies. On this machine, after 30 s, the first island would
hold about 29 trees (133 in 136.6 s) and the other two none. `Island.initialize` raises an
error when an island has no tree (`core.py` of 1.1.0, lines 6894 to 6904).
Even with one tree per island guaranteed, the start would no longer hold the
trees that carry the law. That is not measured.

## The cure of the cause, replayed

**Would a wider fast path change other fits?** `d0_fastpath.jsonl` asks
whether a fast path accepting up to 12 factors (each at most 4, as before)
would change the start of the 81 fits. During the real start of each fit,
every typed draw ran as in 1.1.0, was replayed from the same state of the
random generator with the wider fast path, and the two trees were
compared. The state after the 1.1.0 draw was then put back, so each start
went on as in 1.1.0.

- **The other 80 fits.** Their 31,920 draws give the same trees.
- **III.19.51.** All 50 compared draws differ. 1.1.0 returned None or a tree
  from the blind search after about 0.12 s, on average over its 3,549
  draws. The wider fast path returns at once the product with a free
  coefficient, `-0.092 * v0 * v1 * v1 * v1 * v1 / v2 / v2 / v3 / v3`,
  without n.

**What a fit of III.19.51 returns with it** (`d0_cure.jsonl`). The run is
the one of the U41 campaigns (`phase1_bench.run_one`, 30 s), seeds 0 to 4,
without noise and with 1 % and 10 %. The status and the structure are those
of 1.1 at every seed:

| kind | exact or structure, wider fast path | time | 1.1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| U41 | exact at 5 seeds of 5, in one generation | 1.1 to 1.5 s | exact at 5, 534 to 611 s |
| U41N1 | structure at 5 of 5 | 30 s | structure at 5, 516 to 597 s |
| U41N10 | structure at 5 of 5 | 30 s | structure at 5, 497 to 548 s |

The n² missing from the seeds is found by the search. Every model passes
`check_dimensions`.

## What D0 brings to the author

The slow start has one cause beyond the missing look at the deadline. The
fast path refuses products of more than 8 factors. III.19.51 needs 9, so it
falls to a blind search whose cost has no bound in time. That limit has been
in the code since its first version, with no reason recorded. It matters
only for III.19.51 among the 81 fits of D0.

Two corrections, which can go together:
- **(b1)** The deadline in the typed start, as written in the plan, with at
  least one tree per island so that no island is left empty. Alone, on
  III.19.51 it would most likely lose the exact law: not measured.
- **(b2)** The fast path accepts up to 12 factors. It changes the start of
  III.19.51 only. On that equation it keeps the law at every seed tried,
  with a fit of about a second, or within the 30 s under noise.

Choosing between them is for the author. If (b2) is chosen, the plan's
mechanics of the correction become: III.19.51 under units with
`time_limit=30` ends within the limit and returns a model that passes
`check_dimensions`; every other equation of U41 at seed 0 returns the model
of 1.1.0; the thirteen reference configurations are unchanged. If (b1) is
chosen as well, a test builds a target whose product needs more than 12
factors, and checks that the start ends at the deadline without an empty
island.
