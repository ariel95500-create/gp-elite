Recovering y = 2x + 1 from data with outliers
(RMSE vs the TRUE law on clean points, 5 seeds: median [worst])

   outliers |    MSE (default) |      robust=True | lower median
  --------------------------------------------------------------
         0% |   0.063 [ 0.063] |   0.063 [ 0.063] | tie

        20% |   1.925 [ 1.925] |   1.925 [ 1.925] | tie

  Read the table, not a slogan: robustness costs a little on clean
  data and pays off only when the outliers are numerous enough.
