# Code of conduct

GP_ELITE follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good faith, and keep criticism about the work, not
the person. Bug reports and "it failed on my data" reports are welcome from
everyone, in English or in French, whatever your level.

Conduct concerns go to **ariel95500@gmail.com**. They are read by the
maintainer only and handled confidentially.
