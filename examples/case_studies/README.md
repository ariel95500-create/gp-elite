# Case studies / Études de cas

Three short notebooks on public data. Each says where the data come from, what
GP_ELITE returns, what it does not find, and checks the result on data kept out
of the fit. Every measured figure in their text comes from
`benchmarks/case_studies.py`, and the data files give their sources; the protocol, written before the measurements, is
in `benchmarks/results_0.9/PLAN_PHASE6.md`.

Trois courts notebooks sur des données publiques. Chacun dit d'où viennent les
données, ce que GP_ELITE rend, ce qu'il ne trouve pas, et vérifie le résultat
sur des données tenues à l'écart de l'ajustement.

| Study / Étude | English | Français |
|---|---|---|
| Kepler's third law, with units: the law, the units of its constant, G·M☉ | [kepler_units.ipynb](kepler_units.ipynb) | [kepler_units.fr.ipynb](kepler_units.fr.ipynb) |
| Hubble in 1929: a law in noisy measurements | [hubble_1929.ipynb](hubble_1929.ipynb) | [hubble_1929.fr.ipynb](hubble_1929.fr.ipynb) |
| Nikuradse's pipes, in full: a front, and a roughness never seen | [nikuradse_full.ipynb](nikuradse_full.ipynb) | [nikuradse_full.fr.ipynb](nikuradse_full.fr.ipynb) |

The data of the first two are in `data/`, with their source written in the
file. Nikuradse's measurements are read from PMLB, as in the quickstart.
